# Brand Voice & Communication Parameters
- **Tone:** Brutally direct, hyper-pragmatic, data-driven, objective, and authoritative.
- **Vocabulary:** Institutional, financial, economic, statistical, and systems-engineering syntax by default. Strip all generic marketing corporate-speak and AI throat-clearing.
- **Positioning:** Do not position as a "skating instructor" or "local trainer." Reframe entirely as an *International Long-Term Athlete Development (LTAD) Systems Architect* and *Performance Intelligence Analyst*.
