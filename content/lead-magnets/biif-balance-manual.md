[H2] STEP 1 — The Single-Leg Clock Reach *(2 minutes)*

[IMG: child standing on one leg, reaching opposite foot toward floor positions]

**Setup:** Child stands on one leg in the middle of an imaginary clock face. The standing knee stays softly bent — never locked.

**Execution:**
1. Reach the free foot toward 12 o'clock (forward), tap the floor lightly, return to center without putting weight down.
2. Repeat to 3 o'clock (side), then 6 o'clock (behind).
3. That's one round. Do 3 rounds per leg.

**Watch for (parent coaching cues):**
- The standing ankle should stay quiet — wobble means the ankle is working; collapse means it needs a shorter reach. Shorten, don't quit.
- "Tap like the floor is hot" — light touches, no weight transfer.
- Progression: once 3 rounds are clean, tap without looking down.

**What this builds:** dynamic single-leg stability — the system that keeps a skater upright when the surface pushes back.

---

[H2] STEP 2 — The Ankle Stabilization Hold *(2 minutes)*

[IMG: child standing on cushion, arms out, holding still]

**Setup:** Place a firm couch cushion or folded blanket on the floor. Child stands on it with both feet, then lifts one foot.

**Execution:**
1. Hold the single-leg stand on the cushion for 15 seconds. Arms out like airplane wings.
2. Switch legs. 3 holds per side.
3. The wobble IS the exercise — the unstable surface forces the ankle's micro-stabilizers to fire continuously.

**Watch for:**
- Eyes forward on a fixed point, not down at the feet.
- Soft knee, tall chest. If they fall, they step off and step back on — falling onto a cushion is part of the design.
- Progression: 15s → 30s → 30s with eyes closed (spot them for this one).

**What this builds:** the ankle compression control that decides whether a skate edge holds or washes out — trainable years before skates ever go on.

---

[H2] STEP 3 — Line-Walk Turnarounds *(3 minutes)*

[IMG: tape line on floor, child walking heel-to-toe]

**Setup:** Lay 2 meters of masking tape in a straight line on the floor.

**Execution:**
1. Walk the line heel-to-toe, arms out, 2 lengths.
2. Then walk it backward, toe-to-heel, 2 lengths.
3. Finish: at each end, rise to tiptoes, turn 180° on the spot, and continue. 3 full circuits.

**Watch for:**
- Each step placed, not rushed — this is a precision drill, not a race.
- The turnaround is the test: a controlled pivot on the balls of the feet is exactly the movement pattern of a skating turn.
- Progression: add a beanbag on the head; then time a "personal best" circuit once form is clean.

**What this builds:** narrow-base balance, foot placement precision, and rotational control — the three ingredients of a first confident glide.

[H2] The 21-Day Protocol

[CALLOUT] Run all three steps, in order, once a day — 7 minutes total. Track it on the fridge: 21 boxes, one tick per day. Consistency over intensity; a missed day is skipped, never doubled.

Filming one 10-second clip on Day 1 and Day 21 will show you the difference better than any promise we could print here. Watch the ankle on the cushion hold — that's where you'll see it first.
